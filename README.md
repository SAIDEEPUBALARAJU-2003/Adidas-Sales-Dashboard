#  Adidas Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## 🔍 Project Overview
This project is an **end-to-end Power BI case study** focused on analyzing **Adidas sales performance** using interactive dashboards. The objective is to replicate real-world business reporting by transforming raw sales data into **actionable insights** through effective data modeling, relationships, filters, and visuals.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to understand **sales trends, profitability, product performance, sales channels, and regional insights** to support data-driven decision-making.

---

##  Project Objectives
- Analyze overall Adidas sales and profitability
- Identify **top-performing retailers and cities**
- Track **sales trends over time**
- Compare performance across **product categories and sales methods**
- Perform **regional sales analysis**
- Build an **interactive and business-focused Power BI dashboard**

---

##  Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI**
- **Power Query** (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- **DAX** (KPIs & Calculated Measures)
- **Data Modeling & Relationships**
- **Filters & Slicers**

---

##  Key Metrics & Analysis
1. **Overall Performance Metrics**  
   - Total Sales  
   - Total Profit  
   - Average Price per Unit  
   - Total Units Sold  

2. **Profit Analysis by Retailer**  
   - Identify top-performing retailers and their profit contribution  

3. **Sales Trend Over Time**  
   - Year, quarter, month, and day-level trend analysis  

4. **Product Category Sales Distribution**  
   - Contribution of different product categories to total sales  

5. **Units Sold by Product Category and Gender**  
   - Customer preference analysis based on gender  

6. **Effective Sales Methods Comparison**  
   - Performance comparison across sales channels  

7. **Regional Sales Analysis**  
   - Sales and profit insights across regions, states, and cities  

8. **Top Performing Cities by Profit**  
   - Identification of top 5 cities based on profitability
